About

Research-driven, clinically grounded, and implementation-focused.

I study computer vision and medical AI with an emphasis on deep learning and multi-view learning. My current work includes clinical medical diagnostic assistance, self-supervised learning based on contrastive learning, and robust learning from multiple views.

I am affiliated with EDIL at Hanyang University and HAI at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, and I am pursuing graduate study in Artificial Intelligence Semiconductor.
