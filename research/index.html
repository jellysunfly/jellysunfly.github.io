Research

Research interests centered on reliable multi-view AI.

My work connects computer vision, medical AI, deep learning, and multi-view learning, with an emphasis on clinically useful models and robust representation learning.

Multi-view Learning

Learning from multiple fixed views or poses of the same patient, such as shoulder X-ray views and facial expression views.

🔬

Evidence & Feature Fusion

Fusion strategies for combining complementary view evidence, including hierarchical fusion, confidence-aware alignment, and evidence modeling.
