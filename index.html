About

Research-driven, clinically grounded, and implementation-focused.

My work focuses on bridging deep learning research and practical clinical AI. I am especially interested in how multiple images from the same patient or object can be fused into a more reliable prediction than a single image alone.

This homepage is designed to present research interests, selected projects, publications, technical skills, and CV information in a clean academic style.

Research

Core research interests

A compact overview of the themes that connect my medical AI and multi-view learning projects.

🧠

Multi-view Deep Learning

Learning complementary representations from fixed clinical views such as AP, Axial, Glenoid, Outlet, frontal/lateral, and facial poses.

🔬

Medical Image Analysis

Designing deep learning pipelines for radiography, MRI segmentation, explainability, and clinically meaningful screening support.

✨

Evidence & Feature Fusion

Exploring attention, score fusion, contrastive learning, and Dempster–Shafer evidence aggregation for reliable predictions.
